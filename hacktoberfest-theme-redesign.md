# CommitRush — Editorial Campaign Theme Redesign (Hacktoberfest 2026 Reference)

Based on `/home/charlieputh/Documents/DESIGN.md`.

## Core Guidelines
- **Constraint:** "remember dont change content or anything just theme" — keep all existing content, copy, logic, routing, and data-testids 100% intact.
- **Aesthetic:** Bold, editorial, technical, human, open, premium.
- **Avoid:** Dark neon aesthetics, SaaS gradients, heavy rounded cards (no 20px radius), heavy drop shadows, glowing borders.
- **Palette (Section 7):**
  - Surface: `#ffffff` (white paper)
  - Canvas / Surface Alt: `#f4f4f1` (warm off-white editorial paper)
  - Border: `#d8d8d3` (clean 1px hairline rules)
  - Text Primary: `#111111` (deep charcoal / black)
  - Text Secondary: `#555555`, Text Muted: `#777777`
  - Brand: `#3d5f58` (deep forest green-teal)
  - Accent: `#ff5a1f` (editorial vermilion / orange)
- **Shape & Spacing:**
  - Radius: `2px–8px` (`rounded-sm` or `rounded-md`), NOT heavy 20px rounded cards.
  - Buttons: Sharp rectangular buttons with `2px–6px` radius (`rounded-md` / `rounded-sm`). Primary is solid black `#050505` with white text; secondary is transparent with black border. Pills only for status tags.
  - Hairlines: `1px solid #d8d8d3`.
- **Typography:**
  - Font: Inter / Space Grotesk.
  - Eyebrows: `text-[12px] font-semibold tracking-[0.08em] uppercase text-[#555555]`.
  - Two-part headline emphasis: Plain phrase followed by `<em>` styled phrase.
